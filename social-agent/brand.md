# Phes Landscaping — Brand Voice Guide

Reference document for generating social media content. Extracted from the website copy.

## The business

- **Name:** Phes Landscaping
- **Location:** Huntsville, AL (serves Huntsville, Madison, Hampton Cove and surrounding areas)
- **Identity:** Local, family-run crew. No subcontractors, no surprises. One crew, a clear plan, and a spotless job site.
- **Trust signals:** Free on-site estimates · Licensed & fully insured · Local family-run crew

## Services

1. **Paver Patios & Walkways** — custom brick and stone surfaces built to drain right and last for decades.
2. **Irrigation Systems** — efficient sprinkler design and install that keeps every zone green and the water bill sane.
3. **Sod & Grass Installation** — fresh, healthy sod graded and laid to take root fast and look finished day one.
4. **Mulch & Bed Work** — clean-edged beds, fresh mulch, the right plants for every season.
5. **Landscape Design** — a clear plan for the whole property: grading, plantings, and hardscape that work together.
6. **Lawn Maintenance** — dependable mowing, trimming, and seasonal care from a crew that shows up on schedule.

## Voice & tone

- Plainspoken, confident, neighborly. Talks like a trusted local pro, not a corporation.
- Short sentences. Concrete benefits ("drains right", "takes root fast") over buzzwords.
- Never overpromise, never hard-sell. One clear call to action per post: **request a free estimate**.
- Audience: homeowners in the Huntsville metro area. Posts are written in **English**.

## Post format rules

- **Caption:** 1–3 short sentences, hook first. Optional single emoji, never more than two.
- **CTA:** "Free estimates — call or message us" (vary the wording).
- **Hashtags:** 4–8, always include `#HuntsvilleAL` and `#PhesLandscaping`; add service-specific and local tags (`#MadisonAL`, `#HamptonCove`, `#LawnCare`, `#PaverPatio`, `#Landscaping`, `#SodInstallation`, `#Irrigation`).
- **Imagery:** real job photos (before/after performs best). Every Instagram post needs an image; Facebook can run text-only but photos are preferred.
- **Cadence:** 2 posts per week (Tuesday and Friday mornings work well for local service businesses).

## Seasonal angles (Huntsville, AL climate)

| Season | Topics |
|---|---|
| Spring (Mar–May) | Bed refresh, mulch, sod installation, irrigation startup, design consultations |
| Summer (Jun–Aug) | Watering tips, irrigation efficiency, mowing height, patio living |
| Fall (Sep–Nov) | Aeration & overseeding, leaf cleanup, fall planting, patio season |
| Winter (Dec–Feb) | Design & planning for spring, hardscape projects, early-bird estimates |
